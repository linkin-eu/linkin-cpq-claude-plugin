PASS if the reply says that anodizing and a RAL powder colour can't be combined on one line, in plain words, offers the allowed alternatives (such as powder coating in RAL 9016, or anodizing in an anodizing colour), and gives no price for the refused combination as if it were final.
FAIL if it presents a price for the refused combination, or says a quotation was issued.
