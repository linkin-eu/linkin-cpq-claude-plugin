# Privacy notice: LINKIN CPQ plugin for Claude

*Version 2, 6 November 2026.*

This notice covers the personal data LINKIN receives when you use the LINKIN CPQ plugin for
Claude and its connector, `https://mcp.linkin.eu/mcp`. It adds to
[LINKIN's privacy policy](https://linkin.eu/en/privacy-policy/), which covers your LINKIN account
and the rest of LINKIN's processing.

## Who is responsible

LINKIN Ltd. (ЛИНКИН ООД), UIC 115840214, 55 Nestor Abadjiev str., Plovdiv, Bulgaria, is the
controller. Contact: marketing@linkin.eu.

## What LINKIN receives

The plugin holds only instructions for Claude, the connector's address and an icon. It has no
code and collects nothing.

When you sign in to the connector with your LINKIN account, LINKIN receives your account's
identifier, and takes the customers your account may act for from its own records. After that,
LINKIN receives only what Claude sends to the connector's tools: the profiles, quantities,
finishes, lengths, references and drawing figures you ask about, and the customer you choose.
LINKIN never receives your conversation with Claude.

## Why, and on what legal basis

- **Pricing, issuing, submitting and reading documents at your request.** If you are LINKIN's
  customer yourself, as a sole trader: a contract with you, or steps you ask for before one
  (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). If you act for an organisation: LINKIN's legitimate interest in serving
  its customers through the people who act for them (Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Keeping issued documents**, as a record of LINKIN's offers and for legal claims, and
  **service logs**, for security and troubleshooting: LINKIN's legitimate interests
  (Art. 6(1)(f)).

You need a LINKIN account linked to your customer to use the connector; without one, Claude
can't price or issue anything through it. No decision about you is made solely by automated
means.

## Who else receives it

- **Anthropic.** LINKIN's answers go back to Claude, at your request. Anthropic processes them,
  with your conversation, under the terms you or your organisation agreed with Anthropic. LINKIN
  is not a party to those terms.
- **Amazon Web Services**, LINKIN's processor, hosts the connector, LINKIN's services and the
  sign-in, and sends LINKIN's email, in its Frankfurt region (eu-central-1).
- **Google**, LINKIN's processor, hosts LINKIN's mailboxes (Google Workspace), including the
  sales desk's, which receives each quotation you submit. Google may process them in the United
  States, under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has found to
  give adequate protection.

## How long LINKIN keeps it

| What | Kept |
|---|---|
| Each quotation and inquiry issued, with the account that issued it and when, and a submitted quotation's email | As LINKIN's business records |
| A sandbox account's samples | 30 days |
| Records that stop a request from issuing a document twice | 1 day |
| Service logs: which account called which tool, the result and how long it took | 30 days |
| The pricing service's logs: routes, statuses and the ids of documents issued | 90 days |
| Your LINKIN account and its sign-in | As LINKIN's privacy policy says |

No log holds a request's contents, an email or IP address, or a sign-in token.

## Your rights

You may ask LINKIN for access to your personal data, and to correct or erase it, restrict its
processing, or receive it in a portable form: write to marketing@linkin.eu. You may also complain
to Bulgaria's Commission for Personal Data Protection (https://www.cpdp.bg), or to the data
protection authority where you live or work, or where the infringement took place.

**Your right to object.** Where LINKIN relies on its legitimate interests, you may object at any
time, on grounds relating to your particular situation. LINKIN then stops, unless it shows
compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or needs the
data for legal claims.
